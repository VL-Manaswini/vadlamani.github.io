Research Interests

My research sits at the intersection of computer architecture, VLSI design and electronic design automation, with a focus on energy-efficient AI hardware. Two directions I'm most excited about:

🧩
Direction 01

Energy-Efficient AI Accelerators for Chiplet-Based Architectures

Modern AI accelerators pack compute dies, I/O dies and HBM stacks into one package, and moving data between them costs energy and time. I want to jointly optimize compute organization, interconnects and memory hierarchies to minimize data movement, cut power and speed up inference.

🧠
Direction 02

ML-Driven Physical Design for Chiplet and 3D ICs

Chiplet and 3D-stacked ICs struggle with thermal limits, routing congestion, timing violations and costly design iterations. I want to apply physics-informed graph neural networks, reinforcement learning and formal verification to improve placement, interconnect efficiency, PPA and reliability.
